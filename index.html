School Rules & Regulations

Mu'assasatu Sheikh Muhammad Rabi'u · MSMR Kano

1

Punctuality

All students must arrive on time for every session. Lateness of more than 15 minutes without a valid excuse will be recorded.

2

Respect & Conduct

Students must show respect to all teachers, staff, and fellow students at all times, inside and outside the school premises.

3

Qur'an Handling

The Qur'an must always be handled with respect — in a state of wudhu, placed on a clean surface, and never put on the floor.

4

Daily Memorization

Every student is expected to memorize their daily portion (wird) and present it to their teacher. Consistent failure will be noted.

5

Mobile Phones

Mobile phones must be switched off or on silent during all classes and Qur'an sessions. Unauthorized use is prohibited.

6

Dress Code

All students must dress modestly and appropriately in Islamic attire. No tight, transparent, or inappropriate clothing is allowed.

7

Attendance

Minimum 85% attendance is required. Absences must be reported in advance. Unexplained absences may result in dismissal.

8

Part-Time (Biye) Students

Biye students must attend all Saturday and Sunday sessions without fail. Weekend-only absences follow the same attendance policy.

9

Cleanliness

Students are responsible for keeping their space clean. Littering on school premises is strictly prohibited.

10

Academic Integrity

Cheating, impersonation, or falsifying memorization progress is a serious offence and may result in immediate expulsion.

🤲 By registering, students and their guardians agree to abide by all these rules. The school reserves the right to amend these rules with notice to students and guardians.
